Ronin means wave person.

浪人, rōnin: the characters for wave and person. A samurai with no master, going wherever the road leads.

Ronin does that with a question. No syllabus and no feed deciding for you: pick a thread, follow it as deep as it goes, and the trail is still there when you come back.

Wandering, never lost. Every concept on the map shows the path that led you to it.

Ask once. Follow it down.

Ronin turns a question into a small, expandable map of ideas. Pick a concept to read its context, follow a branch, and come back to the trail later.
